Who We Are

About JusticeVAWA.com

Our mission, values, and commitment to survivors.

Supporting Survivors with
Immediate, Dignified Care

JusticeVAWA.com is a survivor support portal partnering with Emergency Relief Foundation, Inc. We provide emergency housing, relocation support, and essential resources for survivors of domestic violence.

Our team responds with compassion, urgency, and confidentiality — because your safety is our priority.

🛡️ Safety 🌟 Dignity 🔒 Confidentiality 💜 Compassion 💪 Empowerment

Our Mission

Support survivors with immediate access to safe housing, essential resources, and compassionate care — restoring stability, dignity, and hope.

Our Services

Emergency Housing & Shelter Placement

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services and how to get help.

Yes. All services provided through JusticeVAWA.com and Emergency Relief Foundation, Inc. are completely free of charge. You will never be asked to pay for any support we provide.
Absolutely. Your information is only shared with trusted partner agencies involved in directly providing your support services. We will never share your information without your knowledge and consent.
Call 911 immediately. Do not wait to fill out a form. Once you are safe, you can also contact the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (available 24/7) or text "START" to 88788.
We currently serve survivors in California, Nevada, Florida, and Texas. If you are in another state, contact us at info@justicevawa.com and we will connect you with local resources.
Our team reviews all applications within 24 hours. Urgent cases flagged as immediate danger are prioritized. For immediate needs, please call us at (951) 963-0467.
Yes. We provide support for survivors and their children. Indicate that you have children in your application so we can ensure appropriate housing and resources are secured for your whole family.
No documents are required to start. You can apply with just your name and contact information. We understand survivors may not have access to their documents, and we work with you regardless.
We provide referrals to trusted legal aid organizations that can assist with protective orders, family law matters, immigration support, and other legal needs related to domestic violence situations.
